Help

Frequently asked questions

More FAQs →
How do I find where a book is kept?

Open the book's record. The Holdings table shows its call number and status. Use the library layout to find the rack.

The book is checked out. Can I reserve it?

Yes. Log in, open the record and choose Place hold. The library will tell you when it is returned.

How do I renew my books?

Log in and open My account. Select the books and choose Renew. Each loan can be renewed up to 3 times (see the borrowing table for the days added). A book reserved by someone else cannot be renewed.

How do I become a member?

Fill in the membership form, get it signed by the recommending authority, and submit it to the Librarian. You will receive a library card.

I lost my library card or a book. What now?

Report it to the Librarian. A duplicate card costs ₹30. A lost book must be replaced with the current edition, or its recorded price paid three times over if it is out of print. Use the book lost report form.

Where can I find old question papers?

In the Old Question Paper Archive of Knowledge Hub, the college repository.
